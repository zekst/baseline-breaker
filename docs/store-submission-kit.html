2. Google Play, step by step

  1. Create the app in Play Console: name Baseline Breaker, default language English (UK or US), app (not game category yet), free.
  2. Set up Play App Signing. Choose "Use Google-generated key" for the app-signing key and upload our upload-keystore.jks certificate as the upload key. Google then holds the signing key and we keep the upload key; losing the upload key is recoverable, losing an unmanaged signing key is not.
  3. Complete the dashboard tasks: privacy policy URL, app access (no login required), ads declaration (no ads in 1.0), content rating questionnaire (section 5), target audience (13+; do not target children), news app (no), COVID app (no), data safety (section 5), government app (no), financial features (none).
  4. Store listing: paste copy from section 4, upload the icon (512 px), feature graphic (1024 × 500), phone screenshots (at least four), and 7-inch and 10-inch tablet screenshots if tablet support is claimed. Category: Games › Arcade.
  5. Upload the bundle android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab to Internal testing, add tester emails, and install from the opt-in link on at least two real devices.
  6. Closed testing (personal accounts only): 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days, then apply for production access.
  7. Promote to Production with a staged rollout at 20%, watch crash rate and reviews for 48 hours, then 100%.
  8. Review normally completes in 3–7 days for a new app. Common rejections: missing privacy policy, wrong content rating, screenshots showing a different UI. Each is a same-day fix.

3. App Store, step by step

  1. Register the bundle ID in Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles, capabilities: none required for 1.0 (Game Center is added in 1.1).
  2. Create the app record in App Store Connect: name, primary language, bundle ID, SKU (any unique string), user access.
  3. Open the Xcode project ios/App/App.xcodeproj, select the App target, set the Team under Signing & Capabilities, confirm version 1.0 and build 1.
  4. Archive (Product › Archive) with "Any iOS Device" selected, then Distribute App › App Store Connect › Upload. Xcode manages certificates and profiles automatically.
  5. TestFlight: once processing finishes (10–30 minutes), add internal testers (up to 100, no review) and test on at least an older iPhone and a current one. External testers need a brief beta review.
  6. App information: category Games › Arcade, secondary Sports; age rating from the questionnaire (section 5, results in 4+); content rights declaration (no third-party content).
  7. App Privacy: answer "Data not collected" for 1.0. This changes in 1.1 when accounts and analytics arrive.
  8. Version page: screenshots for 6.9-inch and 6.5-inch iPhone and 13-inch iPad (section 4), promotional text, description, keywords, support URL, marketing URL (the web build), the uploaded build, and review notes: "No login required. Drag to move the paddle."
  9. Submit for review. Typical turnaround 24–72 hours. Guideline 4.2 (minimum functionality) is the only realistic risk for a web-technology game; the native splash, haptics, full-screen presentation and offline play address it. If it is raised, reply pointing to those and to the 3D rendering.

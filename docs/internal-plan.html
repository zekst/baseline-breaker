6. Risks and assumptions

  • Real-device performance is the largest unknown. Mitigation: the renderer already drops quality tiers automatically; a 30 fps mode and a "lite" court are in Phase 1.
  • Store review can flag WebView-based games as "minimum functionality" if they look like a wrapped website. Mitigation: native splash, haptics, full-screen, Game Center and Play Games in 1.1.
  • Apple account timing: company enrolment needs a D-U-N-S number; start on day one.
  • Signing key custody: the Android upload key must never be lost. Enrol in Play App Signing so Google holds the app-signing key.
  • Trademark distance: the game is original, but any client branding must avoid tournament and federation marks unless licensed.
  • Estimates assume the current team, the blended rate above, and no change in scope mid-phase. Add 15% contingency on fixed-price quotes.

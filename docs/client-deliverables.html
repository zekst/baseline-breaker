2. What the game does today

30

Hand-built courts

Levels pooled by difficulty and dealt in a fresh order each run, so no two sessions play the same. Multi-hit bricks stack visibly; unbreakable bricks only fall to Smash or Power Shot.

6

Boosts

Defensive Wall, Multiball, Smash, Heavy Ball, Racket XL and Power Shot. A tutorial pass introduces each one in order, then a shuffled deck keeps drops unpredictable.

3D

Stadium presentation

Clay court with chalk lines, tiered stands, sponsor boards, floodlights, real-time shadows, debris on every break, a glowing ball with a trail, and a camera that reacts to play.

×4

Combo scoring

Bricks chained in one rally build a combo and raise the multiplier. Floating score popups, on-court calls ("Game!", "Out!"), personal-best tracking and a share button.

Also included

  • Controls tuned per device: mouse-follow on desktop, drag-anywhere with amplified travel on touch, keyboard support.
  • Haptic feedback on phones, synthesised sound effects with a mute toggle, screen kept awake during play.
  • Automatic quality tiers so the 3D scene stays smooth on mid-range phones.
  • Pause, resume with countdown, results screen, best-score persistence.
  • Accessible focus states, reduced-motion support, portrait guidance on phones.

2. Roadmap deliverables by release

Release 1.0 · ShipWeeks 1–4
  • Real-device QA on six phones and a tablet; performance tiers tuned from measurements; 30 fps fallback.
  • Recorded sound set and short theme replacing the synthesised effects.
  • Analytics and crash reporting live (privacy labels updated accordingly).
  • Store listings, screenshots in every required size, privacy policy hosted.
  • Google Play internal test, closed test if required, production rollout. App Store TestFlight and review.
  • Outcome: Baseline Breaker live on the web, Google Play and the App Store.
Release 1.1 · Play every dayWeeks 4–8
  • Player accounts with Apple, Google and email sign-in; cloud save across web and phone.
  • Global, weekly and friends leaderboards with server-side validation.
  • Daily challenge: one court for everyone each day with its own board and streaks.
  • Endless rally mode and three difficulty settings.
  • Unlockable cosmetics: three courts, five balls, five paddles, three trails.
  • Achievements with Game Center and Play Games; push notifications; share cards.
  • Client branding system: boards, banners, ball, paddle, icon and name from one theme file.
  • Installable web app with full offline play; localisation framework with five languages; accessibility pass; onboarding hint.
  • Automated tests and continuous integration building all three targets.
  • Outcome: a reason to return daily, a measurable funnel, and the brand on the court.
Release 1.2 · GrowWeeks 8–12
  • Rewarded ads (opt-in only) and cosmetic purchases through App Store, Play Billing and web checkout.
  • Seasonal events and remote configuration: themed courts and rewards switched on without an app update.
  • Tournaments: scheduled brackets, fixed courts, prize hooks, sponsor boards.
  • Four new boosts and six boss courts; level editor with shareable court codes.
  • Ghost rally: replay a friend's best run beside your own.
  • Outcome: revenue options and event marketing built into the product.
Later · On demandAfter an audience exists
  • Live head-to-head multiplayer with a real-time server.
  • Controller support and Apple TV / Android TV builds.
